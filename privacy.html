Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

Fueller is the controller of the personal data described here. You can contact us at hello@fueller.app.

What we collect

If you sign up for early access, we collect the email address you send us, plus anything else you choose to include in your message. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and has no forms.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Purpose: to let you know when Fueller launches, and to reply to your message.
  • Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given when you choose to email us to join the list. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Your email passes through our email providers (Amazon Web Services for sending, and Google for receiving and storing mail), who process it on our behalf. This may involve transfers outside the UK, which are covered by those providers' standard safeguards.

How long we keep it

Until we have told you about the launch, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever is sooner. We then delete your address from the list within 30 days. We may keep a copy of your request to be removed, so we do not contact you again.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing hello@fueller.app. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which can see your IP address. We do not receive that information.
